YOUR ANALYTICS GUIDE

Read the numbers. Understand the music.

This website examines historical Spotify chart records. Choose a dashboard below to explore song streams, chart rankings, markets, artists, or weekly albums. These are chart observations, not live Spotify listening totals.

01 · GLOBAL PULSE

What music leads the charts?

Compare recorded song streams, leading tracks, daily activity, and new song entries.

What the numbers mean

Total streams counts recorded plays. Top 10 strength uses chart rank, where lower numbers mean stronger results. New entries counts songs newly recorded on daily charts. Momentum compares similar periods when a comparison is available.

What the visuals show

Top tracks, stream changes through time, a daily stream calendar, and a line chart of new entries.

Open Global Pulse →
02 · REGIONAL RHYTHMS

Which music leads in each country?

Compare artists, record labels, and the share of charted streams associated with each market.

What the numbers mean

Artist volume measures charted streams. Artist count shows how many artists appear in the selected comparison. Market share expresses a percentage of charted streams, not all Spotify listening.

What the visuals show

Local artist ranks, label stream distribution, country-versus-global comparisons, and share over time.

Open Regional Rhythms →
03 · HIT VELOCITY & LONGEVITY

Which songs climb and last?

Explore how songs improve their rank and how many days they stay on the charts.

What the numbers mean

Rank improvement is the distance from entry position to best position. Chart longevity is days on chart. Consecutive streak is the length of an uninterrupted chart appearance.

What the visuals show

Entry-versus-peak rank, time on charts, streak-length distribution, and rank movement for a chosen track.

Open Hit Velocity →
04 · ARTIST DEEP-DIVE

Where does an artist stand?

Search for an artist and inspect their credited chart streams, markets, and charted work.

What the numbers mean

Credited streams assign an equal share of a track's streams to each credited artist. Market reach counts countries appearing in the chart records. Other values depend on the selected artist and available records.

What the visuals show

A map of markets, daily streams, and an available album comparison or leading markets.

Open Artist Deep-Dive →
05 · WEEKLY ALBUM INSIGHTS

Which albums top the charts and stay longer?

Use weekly album rankings without treating album positions as stream totals.

What the numbers mean

Charting albums counts distinct albums, observed weeks counts weekly chart dates, longest run uses reported weeks on chart, and number-one albums counts distinct albums reaching rank 1.

What the visuals show

Best album ranks, weekly album activity, longest chart histories, new entries, and the average staying power of Top 10 albums over time.

Open Weekly Album Insights →
HOW TO EXPLORE

A few useful definitions

Rank #1: first place on a chart. Smaller numbers mean better positions.

Country: the specific chart market, not the listener's residence.

Date range: limits the dates included in the selected dashboard.

Daily vs weekly: song charts use days; album charts use weeks. Their date ranges need not match.

Missing data: a chart or metric disappears when the chosen selection has no supported observations.
